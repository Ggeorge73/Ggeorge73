# AlwaysWomen: Live Shopify Lifestyle Commerce

[Live store](https://alwayswomen.com) · [Repository](https://github.com/Ggeorge73/alwayswomen)

## Executive summary

AlwaysWomen is a live Shopify lifestyle storefront focused on sustainable apparel and accessories. It complements MyWowPet by demonstrating a second e-commerce delivery context: a customer-facing Shopify experience centered on brand presentation, merchandising, discovery, shopping flows, international readiness, and trust.

The site presents sustainable-fashion collections across apparel, clutches, bags, hats, headbands, and scarves. It includes product pricing and availability, cart and account journeys, country/region selection, policy links, and email subscription.

## Product-management lens

AlwaysWomen required product decisions across:

- target customer and sustainable-fashion positioning
- information architecture and category hierarchy
- visual merchandising and promotional emphasis
- trust, policy, and account touchpoints
- international browsing and currency behavior
- prioritization of a polished live experience over broad feature scope

## Portfolio significance
